Privacy Policy

What this app does

GhostGPT is a mobile companion that opens the GhostGPT service. The wrapper itself is designed not to collect or store your chat content.

Network use

When you open the service, your device connects to ghostgpt.live. The service may process information under its own privacy policy.

Your responsibility

Do not enter passwords, API keys, financial data, or other sensitive information unless you trust the service and understand how it is handled.
